## Decisions this plan makes, and why

1. **The bundle is regenerated, never hand-edited.** `pkg/remotemode/web/static/projectors.js` is generated by
   `node scripts/sync-remote-web.mjs` from `frontend/app/view/agents/remoteprojectors.ts`, which bundles
   `transcriptprojection.ts` and `tooldetail.ts`. Task 5 of the run added `toolResultImages` to the projection and did
   not regenerate it, so `pkg/remotemode/web/projectors.test.js` ("the committed bundle is current") fails.
2. **A group of tool calls that holds a card opens in the rendered view.** `groupTimeline` folds three or more
   consecutive tool calls into one closed group (`CollapseRunThreshold`), so a test, diff or image card inside it is
   hidden until clicked. Back-to-back Bash and Read calls are common in a real turn, so most cards would start hidden,
   which defeats the run plan's decision 9 ("tool output cards sit always open under their tool row"). With
   `renderers` set, a burst whose actions include one with a non-null `toolRendererKey` renders open (the same
   "open" mode as the trailing burst while active). A burst with no card still folds, and the cockpit (no `renderers`)
   is unchanged. Why open the whole burst rather than lift the cards out of the fold: a card read without its tool
   row loses what ran.

## Final scenario steps
