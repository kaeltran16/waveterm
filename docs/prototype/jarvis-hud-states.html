Four conditions of the self, ranked. Tone says which; brightness, how far the planes have come out of alignment, how much of it cuts out and how many struts are severed say how badly. Nothing is waiting on you in this row — that is the next axis, and it is deliberately not mixed in here.

Axis 2 · Waiting on you — the posture marker petcondition.ts · PetPosture

Three things can be waiting, and they are told apart by where on the rim the marker sits — never by a count. All three are drawn over an at-rest form, so the only variable in this row is the marker. The form turns further away while it waits, which is study 03's tell; the marker is what makes the difference legible at 132.

The combinations that matter are the ones where two axes reach for the same colour, and the answer this study gives is that the shape survives every one of them while the hue stops carrying information. Cannot-see plus an escalation is an error-red marker on an error-red form: you still read that something is waiting, because the marker is filled and the body behind it is dimmed, but you no longer read that it is a different problem from the register. At-rest plus a review gate is the same sentence in accent blue, and it is the common case rather than the rare one. Register changes ease over about 420ms rather than snapping, the same as production.

Geometry is shared with study 03 (jarvis-hud-form.js), so the two studies cannot drift apart about what the avatar is. The register, posture and moment tables are read straight out of avatarscene.ts and petcondition.ts. Three things here are not straight ports, and they are choices, not oversights:

  • The posture marker keeps production's bearing but study 03's width — 72°, not ±20° — because a ±20° arc stops reading at 132px once the form behind it is dimmed.
  • Production spins the platter faster the healthier the register is. Here the chosen idle already owns stillness at rest, so spin is re-read as a tumble scaled by lost alignment: at-rest contributes none and keeps breathing in place, and only a decaying form moves.
  • Production's ripple travels a network of nodes and links; this form has no network, so the wave is carried by the four concentric parts it does have — core, front ring, mid blades, outer rim.

And one thing this study found rather than decided: the posture marker and the register tone are drawn from the same five theme colours, so two of the twelve pairings are a colour on itself — --color-accent for both at-rest and a review gate, and --color-error for both cannot-see and an escalation. The marker still reads, because it is a filled sector over a form that has been dimmed 58% behind it, but hue has stopped telling you that the two facts are different. The at-rest pairing is the one to care about: it is the state the avatar is in most of the time, waiting on the thing it is asked to wait on most often.
